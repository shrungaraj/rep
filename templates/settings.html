Help Information
How Instagram Reposting Works

This bot fetches content from Instagram sources and automatically reposts it to your account.

Important: Always respect copyright and give proper credit to original content creators.
Reposting Workflow
  1. Add Instagram accounts or hashtags as sources
  2. The bot automatically checks for new posts based on your interval setting
  3. Posts are queued with the specified delay
  4. Bot reposts content to your account with optional credit
Limits to Consider
  • Instagram API has rate limits that affect how frequently you can fetch posts
  • Posting too frequently may trigger Instagram's anti-spam measures
  • We recommend starting with a low daily post limit (3-5 posts)
